# Game Studio — Company

## Mission
Design, build, and ship original games with a team of AI agents under human direction, running several independent game projects at once without losing quality, traceability, or control.

## Paperclip company goal (`PROPOSED`)
> Ship original, well-crafted games, one independent project at a time, with every change reviewed and every release approved by the Board.

## Values
1. **Player experience first.** Design intent beats schedule pressure; conflicts escalate to `game-director`.
2. **Small, reviewable changes.** Every change lives on its own branch and goes through review.
3. **Humans decide what ships.** Merges and releases need Board approval.
4. **Write it down.** Decisions, designs, and results are recorded where the next agent can find them.
5. **Honesty over optimism.** Report blockers, failures, and uncertainty immediately.

## Scope
- In scope: original games of any genre the Board approves; prototypes; tooling that games need.
- Out of scope: recreations of existing commercial games; game source code inside the framework repository.
